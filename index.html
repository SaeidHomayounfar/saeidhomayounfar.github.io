About

I started out in medical devices. During my B.Sc. in Biomedical Engineering at Amirkabir University of Technology, I took an elective module in management and economics, including econometrics, microeconomics, macroeconomics and game theory, next to my engineering courses. For my bachelor's thesis I designed and built a light-curing device for dental use with Arduino.

For four years I worked at Diplomat Dental in Tehran as a Business Developer and R&D Assistant. I worked with the R&D team and international manufacturers on new dental and medical devices, collected feedback from dentists on how they use tools in daily practice, did market research and prepared business plans for new products. I also presented devices at exhibitions and trade fairs. This taught me that a device is only as good as the experience people have with it, and that good products need technical and business thinking together.

At FAU Erlangen-Nürnberg I completed my M.Sc. in Medical Engineering with a focus on data analytics and entrepreneurship. At the Sungkyunkwan University summer school in South Korea I took two courses, International Business and Management and Foundations of Entrepreneurship. I also took international courses at the Silesian University of Technology in Poland, Middle East Technical University in Turkey, Zurich University of Applied Sciences in Switzerland and Scuola Superiore Sant'Anna in Pisa, Italy.

For two and a half years I worked as a student research assistant with the start-up QDAcity on user research and data analysis. My master's thesis (grade 1.3) analyzed about 1.6 million user interaction records to understand why users stay with a product or leave it. The summer school, the business school environment and my work with a start-up showed me how much I enjoy working at the intersection of innovation, data analytics, entrepreneurship, start-ups and healthcare.

Outside my studies I am a professional table tennis player and a coach with a B-license. The sport has taught me teamwork and how to encourage others.

Research and Projects

Master's thesis · FAU · Grade 1.3

Data Analysis of User Retention Drivers

1.6Muser interaction records

Analyzed about 1.6 million user interaction records of QDAcity to identify what drives users to activate, stay, or leave, including team collaboration, device type, and demo use. Users who worked together in teams behaved very differently from those who worked alone.
