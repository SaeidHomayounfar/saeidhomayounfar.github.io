About

My path started with medical devices. During my B.Sc. in Biomedical Engineering at Amirkabir University of Technology, I designed and built a light-curing device using Arduino, and for four years I worked at Diplomat Dental on new dental and medical devices together with international manufacturers. There I learned that a device is only as good as the experience people have with it.

At FAU Erlangen-Nürnberg, this interest moved from devices to digital products. I completed my M.Sc. in Medical Engineering with a specialization in data analytics and entrepreneurship, and for two and a half years I worked on user research and data analysis for QDAcity, a qualitative data analysis tool. My master's thesis (grade 1.3) analyzed about 1.6 million user interaction records to understand why users stay with a product or leave it.

Today I am interested in how people experience and adopt new technologies, and how empirical research can make products more useful and more human.

Research and Projects

Master's thesis · FAU · Grade 1.3

Data Analysis of User Retention Drivers

1.6Muser interaction records

Analyzed about 1.6 million user interaction records of QDAcity to identify what drives users to activate, stay, or leave, including team collaboration, device type, and demo use. Users who worked together in teams behaved very differently from those who worked alone.
